# TFTG Roster

Monthly shift roster for the TFTG stores (Ascomo, Florida, Porac, Resettle, Caloocan).

Open the site, type each person's requested day offs, and it builds the schedule:

- Florida and Porac: 1–2 openers and 2 closers. Ascomo and Resettle: 1 opener and 1 closer. Caloocan: 1 opener, 1–2 closers, plus a weekly mid-shift rotation. A Straight (S) shift counts as both an opener and a closer.
- A Straight always comes with an opener: a 3-person day is S-O-C or O-C-C, never S-C-C, and extra people open (up to the store's most openers) before they close.
- Straight is capped per person: at most 2 a week and 6 a month (both set in Setup), and it takes turns so it is shared fairly. When everyone at a store has used theirs, the store gets 1 closer that day (marked yellow); someone working alone still gets Straight, and if that goes over the limit the day is marked red.
- Ascomo's extra staff close at Florida; if Ascomo has nobody, Jun or Jonas covers from Resettle.
- Holidays run like a normal day: each store keeps its usual opener and closers, and Straight is only given as usual. Anyone extra gets the holiday off, and regular and special holidays take turns separately so those days off are shared evenly. Holiday Straights also take turns: nobody gets Straight on two holidays in a row (if it can't be avoided, the day is marked red), and holiday Straights, openings and days off go to whoever has had the fewest so far this year, carried over from earlier months.
- "Absent today" marks a sudden absence: only that day is re-planned, and the Schedule page lists who must change shift.

No key or PIN is needed. Data saves in the browser on each device. Optionally, link the "TFTG Roster Data" Google Sheet (Setup › Google Sheet backup) to keep a copy there; `apps-script/Code.gs` is the script that goes in the sheet.
